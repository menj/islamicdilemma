Sources and scholarship

Everything this site cites, so you can check the claims yourself.

We cite the Arabic text, classical commentaries and Christian scholarship by name. Where a source is online, it is linked.

Qur’an texts

TanzilText

Verified Arabic text of the Qur’an in several scripts, with translations for comparison.

Quran.comText

Arabic with word-by-word glosses and multiple English translations.

Corpus CoranicumManuscripts

Berlin-Brandenburg Academy project documenting early Qur’an manuscripts and textual variants.

Classical commentary

al-Ṭabarī, Jāmiʿ al-BayānTafsir

The earliest large commentary, preserving reports from the Companions on verses like 5:48.

Ibn Taymiyyah, al-Jawāb al-ṢaḥīḥPolemic

A fourteenth-century reply to Christian apologetics. Partial English translation by Thomas F. Michel (1984).

Altafsir.comTafsir

Searchable Arabic texts of the major classical commentaries.

The Bible and its history

Codex Sinaiticus ProjectManuscripts

Full images and transcription of the fourth-century Greek Bible manuscript.

ESV BibleText

The translation quoted on this site.

Bruce M. Metzger, A Textual Commentary on the Greek New TestamentScholarship

Standard reference on New Testament textual variants, by a Christian scholar.

Using these sources

Quote verses with their surah and verse number, in Arabic where possible, and check any English rendering against the original.
