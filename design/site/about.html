What this site is

islamicdilemma.info is a Muslim reply to the argument known as the “Islamic Dilemma”.

Editorial standards

  • Every verse is quoted in Arabic with its reference before any translation.
  • Verses are read with the passage around them, not in isolation.
  • The opposing argument is stated as its advocates make it, before we answer it.
  • Sources are cited by work and section so readers can check them.

Corrections

If we have misquoted a source or misstated the argument, tell us and we will correct it and note the change at the end of the page.
